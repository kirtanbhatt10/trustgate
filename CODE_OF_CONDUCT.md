# Code of conduct

trustgate follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, critique code rather than people, and
make it easy for newcomers to take part. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not
accepted anywhere in the project: issues, pull requests, discussions or other channels.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/kirtanbhatt10). Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break
these rules.
